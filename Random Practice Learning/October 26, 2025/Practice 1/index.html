vh vs dvh and svh

The vh is not dynamic and will look for the screen size no matter adressbar or navigation bar. It will have a scroll of the part that covers the naviagation and adress bar. not dynamic

The dvh is dynamic and will adjust to the screen size even with the adressbar and navigation bar. It will not have a scroll of the part that covers the naviagation and adress bar. dynamic

the svh will cut the part of the address bar and the navigation bar. When we scroll on mobile the address bar hides, but it will result in less area covering as it is not dynamic.
